## Description:
In this nursery simulation game, a player will manage every aspect of their own nursery, from the purchase of plants to their placement, care, and sale. 
* Buy upgrades to help your plants grow, and create a flourishing business.
* You only have two hands... hire staff to help take care of your plants and manage impatient customers. 
* Make sure to take good care of both your plants and your customers to avoid losing funding for your business!

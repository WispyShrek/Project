A concrete payment strategy for Electronic Funds Transfer (\mbox{\hyperlink{classEFT}{EFT}}). 

This class inherits from \mbox{\hyperlink{classPaymentStrategy}{Payment\+Strategy}} and implements the {\ttfamily pay} method to handle payments made via \mbox{\hyperlink{classEFT}{EFT}}.

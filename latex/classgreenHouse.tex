\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A class representing a greenhouse that holds a 3x3 grid of plants. 

The \mbox{\hyperlink{classgreenHouse}{green\+House}} class inherits from the \mbox{\hyperlink{classCollection}{Collection$<$\+Plant$\ast$$>$}} class and implements methods to manage a 3x3 grid of \mbox{\hyperlink{classPlant}{Plant}} pointers, including adding, removing, and iterating over the plants in the greenhouse.

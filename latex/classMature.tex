\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A class representing the mature state of a plant, derived from the \mbox{\hyperlink{classPlantState}{Plant\+State}} base class. 

The \mbox{\hyperlink{classMature}{Mature}} class implements the behavior of a plant that has reached maturity. In this state, the plant can transition to other states based on care and conditions.

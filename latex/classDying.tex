\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A class representing the dying state of a plant, derived from the \mbox{\hyperlink{classPlantState}{Plant\+State}} base class. 

The \mbox{\hyperlink{classDying}{Dying}} class implements the behavior of a plant that is in the process of dying. In this state, the plant may not transition to any other state unless specific care is applied.

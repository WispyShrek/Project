\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A class representing the flowering state of a plant, derived from the \mbox{\hyperlink{classPlantState}{Plant\+State}} base class. 

The \mbox{\hyperlink{classFlowering}{Flowering}} class implements the behavior of a plant that is in the flowering stage. In this state, the plant can transition to the \mbox{\hyperlink{classMature}{Mature}} state upon receiving care.

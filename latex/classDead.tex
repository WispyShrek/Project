\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A class representing the dead state of a plant, derived from the \mbox{\hyperlink{classPlantState}{Plant\+State}} base class. 

The \mbox{\hyperlink{classDead}{Dead}} class implements the behavior of a plant that has reached the end of its lifecycle. Once in the \mbox{\hyperlink{classDead}{Dead}} state, the plant cannot transition to any other state.
